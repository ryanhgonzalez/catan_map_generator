Setup 5

Who goes first

Everyone rolls both dice; the highest roll starts.

Placing your first pieces

In turn order, each player places a settlement and a road touching it. Then the last player goes again and play runs back the other way, so each player places a second settlement and road.

Your second settlement

It can go anywhere the distance rule allows and doesn’t need to connect to your first. Its road must touch it.

Starting resources

Take 1 resource for each land hex touching your second settlement. The last player to place their second settlement takes the first turn.

The robber

It starts the game on the desert.

A turn 4

Roll for resources

Roll both dice. Every settlement next to a hex showing that number collects 1 of its resource, and every city collects 2. The hex with the robber produces nothing.

Trade, then build

After rolling, trade, then build. Once everyone knows the game, the rulebook suggests combining the two: trade and build in any order, as often as you like.

One development card

Play at most 1 per turn, at any point in your turn, even before you roll. It can’t be one you bought this turn.

When the bank runs out

If there aren’t enough of a resource for everyone it’s owed to, no one gets that resource this turn. If only one player is owed it, they take what’s left.

Building 6

Your pieces

Each player has 15 roads, 5 settlements and 4 cities, and can’t build more than that.

Roads

One road per edge. A new road must join one of your roads, settlements or cities.

Settlements

Built on an empty corner touching one of your roads, following the distance rule. Worth 1 point.

Distance rule

None of the 3 corners next to a settlement can have a settlement or city on it, yours included.

Cities

Upgrade one of your settlements; the settlement goes back to your supply. Worth 2 points, and collects 2 resources per roll.

Development cards

Take the top card and keep it hidden. Used cards never go back into the deck.

Development cards 5

Knight

Move the robber and steal, just as on a 7. Played knights stay face up in front of you and count toward Largest Army. There are 14.

Road Building

Place 2 roads for free, following the usual rules.

Year of Plenty

Take any 2 resources from the bank. You can use them this turn.

Monopoly

Name a resource. Every other player gives you all of that resource they hold.

Victory Point

Worth 1 point; keep it hidden. Reveal your Victory Point cards when they take you to 10 on your turn, even the turn you bought one. There are 5.

The robber 3

Rolling a 7

No one collects resources. Anyone holding more than 7 resource cards (8 or more) discards half of them, rounded down.

Moving the robber

Whoever rolled must move it to a different hex (the desert counts), then steals 1 random resource from a player with a settlement or city on that hex. If there are several, they choose who.

A blocked hex

The hex under the robber produces nothing until the robber moves again.

Trading 4

With other players

On your turn, trade with anyone, on any terms you agree. You must be part of every trade; other players can’t trade with each other.

Not allowed

Giving cards away, trading like for like (say, 3 ore for 1 ore), trading development cards, or trading with the bank on someone else’s turn.

With the bank

On your turn, trade 4 of one resource for 1 of any other, harbor or not.

Harbors

With a settlement or city on a harbor: at a 3:1 harbor trade 3 of a kind for 1 of any other; at a 2:1 harbor trade 2 of its pictured resource for 1 of any other.

Longest Road & Largest Army 3

Longest Road

The first player with a continuous road of 5 or more segments takes this card, worth 2 points. Only the single longest branch counts. A player who builds a longer road takes it away.

Breaking a road

A settlement built on an empty corner along someone’s road splits it. If the holder is still tied for longest, they keep the card. If others tie for longest, or no one has 5 or more, the card is set aside until one player has the longest.

Largest Army

The first player with 3 knights face up takes this card, worth 2 points. A player with more knights face up takes it away.

Winning 2

10 points on your turn

Reach 10 points during your own turn and you win at once. If you get there on someone else’s turn, you win once your turn comes.

Points

Settlement 1, city 2, Longest Road 2, Largest Army 2, and 1 for each Victory Point card.

5–6 players 3

Paired turns

The starting player is player 1, and the third player to their left is player 2. Player 1 rolls and takes a full turn. Then player 2 builds, trades with the bank only, and may play a development card. Then both pass their markers to the left.

If both reach 10

Player 1 wins.

Older boxes

Before 2022, 5–6 player games used a special building phase instead: after each turn, everyone else could build in turn order, but not trade.
