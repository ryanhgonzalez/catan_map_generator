About

Boards respect Catan tile, number and harbor distributions for both standard and expanded maps, and follow the balance rules you pick. Use the arrows to step through boards you've generated, and the share button to send a link to the board on screen.

Install Catan Board Generator

Add it to your home screen for one-tap, full-screen access. It works offline too, so it's ready at the table.

  1. Tap Share in your browser's toolbar
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen
